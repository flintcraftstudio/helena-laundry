# Impeccable — Design Guidelines for Helena Laundry

> One-time design context captured for all future sessions. Source of truth for
> tokens is `Helena Laundry Design System/colors_and_type.css` and
> `tailwind/tailwind.config.js` (`hl-*` prefix). Source of truth for voice is the
> `helena-laundry-brand-voice` skill + `helena-laundry-voice.md`.

## Design Context

### Users

Two audiences, one site that points at a single action — **book a pickup**.

- **Helena Valley residents** — busy households who want laundry off their plate.
  They're reading on a phone, mid-errand, deciding whether to trust one local
  person with their clothes. The job: *"get this chore handled by someone who
  actually cares how my towels come out."*
- **Local businesses** — salons, spas, short-term rentals, gyms going through
  towels and linens by the armful. They want a standing pickup, predictable bulk
  rates, and one reliable person who knows their order. The job: *"stop losing
  hours to laundry and trust it gets back on time."*

Context of use: warm-toned, local, low-pressure. Helena has a large Air Force
community and many transplants — the site celebrates *local, physical, human
work* without ever resenting newcomers (the owner was an out-of-state military
spouse herself).

### Brand Personality

**A spunky, no-nonsense Montana laundress — Beth Dutton business sense fused with
Mrs. Weasley warmth.** Direct, capable, warm-with-a-backbone. Three words:
**capable · neighborly · honest.**

- **Voice is first-person singular "I"** — she's one real person, and that's the
  moat against faceless national apps. Named **Chanté** (accented é, always) on
  the About page only; the landing page stays first-person but unnamed.
- **Lead with the answer, then the warmth.** Say the price out loud, no apology.
  Specific over vague ("back on your porch in 24–48 hours," not "fast turnaround").
- Warmth shows as *competence and care*, never gushing or saccharine. Dry humor
  once per piece, rationed exclamation points, light Montana/Irish texture (not
  costume).
- **Do NOT use the Firefly "we" voice, and do NOT use the design-system README's
  older "we" sample copy.** Use the copy in `helena-laundry-site-copy.md` verbatim.
- Emotional goal: a visitor should feel *"she's clearly good at this, and she
  cares — I'd hand her my laundry."*

### Aesthetic Direction

**Warm vintage-Americana, light theme, clean modern execution.** Bright, human,
tactile — never corporate, sterile, or cold. Light mode only.

- **The hero is the badge.** A circular Art-Nouveau medallion — auburn-haired
  woman in a floral headscarf, flexing (a neighborly Rosie-the-Riveter) — at
  `web/static/img/badge.{webp,png}`, framed by a CSS double-ring echoing its
  border. She does the heavy lifting; keep everything around her simple and roomy.
- **Palette (`hl-*` tokens):** cream `#F2EBD8` is the page (never stark white as
  base; white is for cards that lift off cream). Rust `#C0492B` is the primary
  accent (buttons, links, key headings); teal `#3E7E8C` secondary (eyebrows,
  icons, alt sections); ochre `#D99A3D` a sparing pop; brown `#3A2A1E` for ALL
  text and linework — **never pure black, neon, cold gray, or corporate blue.**
  Roughly 60% cream/white · 30% one accent · 10% the other + ochre.
- **Type:** DM Serif Display (warm high-contrast serif) for display/headings;
  DM Sans for body/UI. Sentence case everywhere; ALL-CAPS only for tiny tracked
  teal eyebrow labels (`WASH · FOLD · DELIVER`). Generous line-height (1.6 body).
- **Shape & texture:** soft rounded corners (cards 16px, pill buttons, insets
  8–12px), thin rust/brown rules and the signature **double-ring** device,
  brown-tinted soft shadows (never harsh black), flat cream backgrounds (no busy
  patterns or heavy gradients), floral headscarf accents only as a rare corner
  flourish.
- **Icons:** inline SVG templ components (Lucide-style, 1.75–2px round-cap stroke).
  **No Lucide CDN / JS dependency** — the design-system README's CDN note is
  superseded. No emoji as iconography.
- **Anti-references:** SaaS landing pages, greeting cards, faceless laundry apps,
  cold stock photography, corporate jargon, urgency/FOMO, exclamation-point spam.

### Resolved Direction (from setup Q&A)

- **Lead emotion in a trade-off: trust & competence.** When warmth and
  reliability compete, lean toward looking capable, confident, and worth the
  price — cleaner and more self-assured over cozier and more decorative. The
  warmth is always present (it's the brand), but competence wins the tie.
- **Motion: restrained, with ONE signature moment.** Default to the spec — soft
  fades, 8–12px rises on scroll-in, tactile button press (≈0.97 scale, slight
  darken), easing `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`, 140–420ms. Allow exactly one
  memorable beat: the **badge double-ring drawing in on load.** No parallax, no
  infinite looping decoration, nothing that fights the "unhurried" brand. Always
  honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA.** All text meets AA contrast. **Restrict ochre
  `#D99A3D` and light teal `#3E7E8C` to decorative or large-text use only** —
  never small body text on cream. Keep brown text on cream/white for body.
  Maintain the existing rust focus-visible outline and reduced-motion handling.

### Design Principles

1. **One real person, said out loud.** Every screen should feel like it comes
   from Chanté — first-person, direct, the price stated plainly. The human is the
   product; never let the UI flatten her into a faceless company.
2. **Cream breathes; color concentrates.** Keep large surfaces calm and roomy
   (64–128px section padding, ~62ch line length). Pour color into accents —
   buttons, links, key headings, the one hero band per page. Whitespace is what
   keeps the warm palette clean.
3. **Competence first, warmth always.** When in doubt, look capable and
   confident — that's what earns the price and the trust. Then let the warmth
   show through care and specificity, not decoration or gush.
4. **Tactile, not flashy.** Soft rounded corners, brown-tinted shadows, gentle
   hand-friendly motion. One signature beat (the badge ring), and no more. Calm
   and unhurried beats clever.
5. **Honest and accessible by default.** Plain numbers, no FOMO, AA contrast,
   reduced-motion respected. Montana-modest in both copy and craft.
